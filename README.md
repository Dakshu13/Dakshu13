### The Sorting Hat's verdict

I am an MSc Computing (AI and NLP) graduate based in Dublin. If the Sorting Hat had to place me, it would choose whichever house values careful evidence. I build data pipelines, and I care about whether an AI system is actually good, not just whether one score looks high. My dissertation covered Tamil and Malayalam, two languages with very few evaluation resources.

### Defence Against the Dark Arts: one finding I like

> In my dissertation, the simple baseline scored **0.745** on ROUGE L but came **last** with native speaker readers (2.31 to 2.40 out of 5). MuRIL retrieval with GLM scored lowest on overlap but **highest** with readers (4.45 to 4.48). One number can mislead, so I measure in two ways.
